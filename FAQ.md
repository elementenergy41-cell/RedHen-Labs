# RedHen Labs — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about [RedHen Labs](https://rrw-ads.com), an Amazon seller profit-tracking and PPC automation platform covering Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display. See also the [README](README.md), [GLOSSARY](GLOSSARY.md), and [COMPARISON](COMPARISON.md).

## Product

### What does RedHen Labs do?
It calculates your real net profit per product on Amazon — revenue minus cost of goods (COGS), Amazon referral and FBA fulfillment fees, ad spend, and refunds — and uses that to drive PPC automation across Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands (rule-based and AI-assisted), keyword harvesting, negative-keyword management, and automated review requests. Sponsored Display syncs and reports alongside them and has its own AI bid engine. It is a focused profit-and-PPC platform, not an all-in-one suite.

### Is RedHen Labs a "cut my ad spend" tool?
No. It manages ACoS in both directions. On mature products you can defend margin; on products where the math supports it, you can lean in and invest for share. The metric that matters is real profit and TACoS (total ad cost of sales) over time — not minimizing ACoS for its own sake.

### Is this just an AI wrapper?
No. The foundation is real profit accounting and rule-based automation you configure and control. AI recommendations are a supporting tool — every suggestion shows its reasoning and confidence, and nothing is applied until you approve it unless you deliberately hand a specific engine permission to act on its own. Auto-apply ships off on every plan: on Growth you may let an individual AI profile apply its own bid changes, and on Professional you may do the same for the rule engine.

### Will anything change in my campaigns without my approval?
That depends on your plan, and you decide. Nothing runs unattended unless you switch it on yourself — every automation and AI profile ships with auto-apply off, and recommendations wait in a queue until you approve or dismiss them. On Review ($19/mo) there is no PPC at all, so nothing can reach your campaigns. On Growth ($69/mo) you can hand an individual AI profile the keys to apply its own bid changes, one profile at a time. On Professional ($129/mo) you can do the same with the rule-based automations. Sponsored Display recommendations are the one exception: they always wait for your approval, on every plan including Professional. Whatever you leave off keeps waiting for your approval, bid floors and ceilings bound every change, and there is a full audit trail and one-click undo on every change either way.

### Which Amazon ad types are supported?
Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display. All three sync into your dashboard, so ad spend, ACoS and real profit count every campaign type — on every paid plan, including Review ($19/mo). Optimization differs by type. Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands get the rule automations: bid rules, negative keywords (written at ad-group level on Sponsored Brands, the only level Amazon accepts there), search-term harvesting, budget automation and dayparting. Placement bid modifiers are Sponsored Products only. Sponsored Display gets AI bid recommendations from its own dedicated engine, and those always wait for your approval — on every plan, including Professional. On Growth and Professional, Sponsored Display campaigns also get their own table where you can pause, activate and set daily budgets.

### What does RedHen Labs intentionally NOT do?
It does not offer lifetime-value, repeat-purchase, or subscription analytics, and it does not promise specific ACoS or revenue outcomes. The scope is narrow on purpose: profit tracking, Amazon PPC automation, and review automation.

## Pricing

### How much does RedHen Labs cost?
Flat monthly pricing, never a percentage of ad spend:
- **Review — $19/mo:** real profit per product, unlimited products tracked, automated review requests. No PPC automation. 14-day free trial.
- **Growth — $69/mo (~$58/mo annual, $704/yr):** everything in Review plus full PPC management across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, AI bid recommendations with reasoning, campaign wizards including campaign chaining with keyword isolation, and search-term and negative-keyword suggestions. Unlimited products. Changes wait in a review queue for your approval, or you can switch an individual AI profile to apply its own bid changes unattended. Rule-based automation — bid rules, negatives and harvesting — is Professional. 14-day free trial.
- **Professional — $129/mo (~$110/mo annual, $1,321/yr):** everything in Growth, plus the **rule engine** — rule-based bid automation, negative-keyword and search-term harvest automation, budget and placement automations, and inventory guards, each of which you either queue for approval or let run unattended — as well as dayparting (bids raised or reduced by hour of day and day of week, with the original bid restored when the window closes) and the plain-English AI assistant. Full audit trail with one-click undo. 14-day free trial.
- **Operator Setup — $399 first month, then $129/mo:** done-for-you setup plus a written 30-day operating runbook.

**Every paid plan tracks unlimited products.** The difference between Growth and Professional is which automation engines you get — Growth's AI recommends and, if you switch it on, applies bid changes on its own; Professional adds the rule engine, dayparting and the plain-English assistant. It is not a product count, and it is not whether the platform can act unattended.

### What's included in the $19 Review plan?
Real profit per product (after COGS, fees, ad spend, and returns), unlimited product tracking, and automated Amazon review requests. It includes a 14-day free trial, then $19/mo. It does **not** include PPC features — for campaign setup and AI recommendations, which you can approve one by one or let the AI apply for you, upgrade to Growth ($69/mo); for bid rules, negative-keyword and harvest automation, dayparting, and the assistant you can ask questions in plain English, that's Professional ($129/mo).

### Do you charge a percentage of my ad spend?
No, ever. The subscription is the whole bill — the same at $5k of monthly ad spend and at $500k. Percentage-of-spend pricing is common among agencies and now appears in software pricing too; RedHen Labs does not use it, because a fee that rises with your spend, attached to software that raises your spend, is a structure worth understanding before you agree to it.

### What should Amazon PPC management cost — agency vs. software?
There is no single norm worth quoting. Agency fee structures vary widely — a percentage of ad spend, a percentage of ad-attributed sales, a flat management fee, or a base plus a percentage are all common, and a percentage of ad spend has started appearing in software pricing too. The structure matters less than what the fee works out to against the profit your advertising actually produces, which is the distance between your break-even ACoS and your actual ACoS. Software is a fixed line on the other side of that comparison: RedHen Labs starts at $19/mo for profit tracking + review automation, $69/mo adds full PPC management and AI bid changes you approve or let run on their own, and $129/mo adds the rule engine and the plain-English assistant — flat, never a percentage of spend.

### How much should AI-driven Amazon PPC cost for a $500k–$2M revenue seller?
On a percentage-of-spend model, a seller at that revenue can easily pay $500–$2,000+ per month as ad budgets scale. RedHen Labs stays at $129/mo flat at any spend level, so your cost doesn't climb with your budget — the savings grow as you scale.

### Is there a free trial?
Yes — **all three plans include a 14-day free trial**. **A card is required to start**, but you pay $0 today and can cancel anytime before day 14 at no charge.

### What happens after my 14-day trial?
Your card on file is charged your plan price (Review $19 / Growth $69 / Professional $129) on day 14 and the subscription renews monthly from there. You can cancel anytime before day 14 at no charge, and anytime afterwards to stop the next renewal. Cancelling does not delete your data — you can pick up where you left off.

### Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings; changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

### Is annual billing available?
Yes. Annual billing saves roughly 15% on Growth and Professional.

### What does Operator Setup include?
The team reviews your catalog, builds your campaign architecture, configures rules and automation, sets up profit tracking with your COGS, and delivers a written 30-day operating framework. You receive a fully running system, then continue at $129/month.

## Accounts, data & security

### How many Amazon accounts can I connect?
Every plan includes one Amazon Seller Central account. To manage multiple accounts, [contact us](https://rrw-ads.com/support) or ask about the partner program.

### Which marketplaces are supported?
United States, Canada, Mexico, and Brazil.

### Is my Amazon data secure?
Yes. RedHen Labs uses encryption in transit and at rest, never sells your data, and complies with Amazon's SP-API data-protection requirements. Your data is shared only with the service providers that run the product — the database and application hosts, and, when you use an AI feature, Anthropic, which processes the request and does not train on it. You connect via OAuth (no password sharing) and can revoke access with one click.

### How does RedHen Labs connect to Amazon?
Through OAuth authorization to Amazon's Advertising API and Selling Partner API (SP-API). Data is synced nightly into a database; the platform never makes decisions on stale or partial live API calls. A 14-day attribution correction re-syncs recent data to capture delayed conversions.

## Comparisons

### How is this different from Helium 10 or Perpetua?
RedHen Labs doesn't bundle 20 tools you'll never use, and it doesn't charge a percentage of ad spend. It is one focused platform: real profit-and-loss per product, AI recommendations with their reasoning shown, rule-based automation you control — approve each change or let it run unattended — and flat-rate pricing. See [COMPARISON.md](COMPARISON.md) for detail.

### Should I use software or hire an agency?
If you want the management workflow without paying an agency's management fee on top of your ad spend, software you run yourself is usually the cheaper path — and you set the level of control, from an approval gate on every change to automations that run on their own. Operator Setup bridges the two: done-for-you setup, then you operate it.
